Player

A thick seek bar. Thin everything else.

The picture gets the screen. Controls stay slim until you need them, and the seek bar is big enough to hit the first time, with a preview of where you are going.

  • Preview thumbnails and chapter marks on the seek bar, optionally a waveform
  • Resume where you stopped, speed, A-B repeat and a sleep timer that fades out
  • 10-band equalizer and a subtitle style editor
  • Picture-in-picture and a queue you can reorder

Hover the seek bar: preview, chapter name and time.

TV & radio

Switch on the world.

Public TV channels and radio stations are built right in, sorted by language and shown with their logos. Pick a flag, pick a station, done.

  • What's on now next to every channel, and the program for today and tomorrow
  • Radio shows the song that is playing, in the player and the mini player
  • Favorites and search for channels and stations
  • Music and radio keep playing while you browse

Android

Just as calm in your hand.

On the phone Velour gets a layout made for thumbs: big targets, a bottom bar and a player you control with gestures.

  • Swipe for volume and brightness, double-tap the edges to skip
  • Lock the screen while watching
  • Arrange the player bar live, with drag and drop
  • A mini player keeps music and radio going while you look around

Questions

Good to know.

Is Velour really free?

Yes. Velour is open source under the GPL-3.0. There is no account, no subscription and no advertising.

Where do the TV channels and radio stations come from?

From public directories: TV channels from iptv-org, program guides from epgshare01 and radio stations from Radio Browser. Velour plays the streams that broadcasters publish and does not host any itself. Which channels work depends on the broadcaster; some are only available in their own country.

Do I need Plex?

No. Plex is optional. Velour plays your local files, network streams, TV and radio without it. If you have a Plex server, sign in and it shows up in the library.

What does Velour connect to?

GitHub once a day to look for updates (you can turn this off). The TV and radio directories, program guides, logos and flags only when you open those pages. Your Plex server if you sign in. There is no tracking.

macOS or Linux?

Not at the moment. Velour is made for Windows and Android.
